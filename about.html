About Income Spectrum

What this site is, how it works, and how we disclose affiliate relationships.

What Income Spectrum Is

Income Spectrum is a directory of independent income options, education and training resources, business support services, and official state and federal self employment information. The site is organized as a database-style reference for aspiring and established self-employed and small business operators exploring ways to earn income, build skills, access business support, or find official self employment information.

The site covers four main areas:

  • Income Options - Platforms, marketplaces, and models for service-based income, selling and making products, business ownership, and recurring income
  • Education and Training Resources - Job-skill training, business education, digital skills, certifications, and exam preparation
  • Supportive Services - Finance, legal, operations, marketing, advisory, and business transaction services
  • State and Federal Resources - Official government business information, registration links, EIN and tax resources, and compliance references for all 50 states and DC

How Listings Are Selected

Listings are selected based on relevance to the directory category, general reputation, and availability to a broad U.S. audience. Income Spectrum does not verify, audit, endorse, or guarantee any listed company, platform, service, or opportunity.

Inclusion in the directory is not a recommendation. Users should conduct their own research before using any listed platform, service, or resource. Nothing on this site constitutes legal, financial, tax, business, investment, or professional advice.

Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on Income Spectrum are affiliate links. This means that if you click a link and make a purchase or sign up, Income Spectrum may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate links are marked with rel="sponsored" in the HTML. Affiliate relationships do not influence which listings are included in the directory or how they are described. We do not accept payment for directory placement.

Income Spectrum is not a job board, a job placement service, or an investment platform. It is not affiliated with any government agency. The state and federal resource pages link to official government websites but are not operated by or affiliated with those agencies.

Income Spectrum does not guarantee income results, job placement, business success, or any specific outcome from using any listed resource.
