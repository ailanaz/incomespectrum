Trades and Technical

Physical skills and technical trades with low overhead, high repeat demand, and no traditional storefront required.

Mobile Auto Detailing

Origin: USA Auto Trades
Auto Trades

People pay $150 to $400 to not take their car somewhere. No shop, no lease, no walk-ins. A van, supplies, and a booking app.

Economics

Startup range $2,000 to $8,000. A full day of appointments runs $600 to $1,200.

Mobile Trailer Bar

Origin: Australia Events and Hospitality
Events and Hospitality

A fully stocked bar on a trailer that rents out for weddings and events. The client provides the liquor. The operator provides the setup, the service, and the look. Widely common in Australia, rare in the US.

Economics

$800 to $3,000 per event. A busy summer calendar with 8 to 12 events per month is a full business.

Food and Agriculture

Food-based and farm-based businesses built around direct sales, recurring markets, and low-overhead production. Cottage food laws in most states allow baking and candy making to start from a home kitchen legally.

Food and Mobile

Truck purchase or conversion runs $20,000 to $100,000. High-volume city locations and event contracts are the primary revenue drivers.

Agriculture

Small acreage producing high-value crops like salad greens, herbs, and specialty vegetables sold direct to restaurants or at farmers markets. The direct-to-buyer model removes the middleman and keeps margins high.

Farmers Market Vendor

Origin: USA Direct Sales
Direct Sales

A booth-based business built around a single product category. Produce, baked goods, honey, plants, preserves. Weekly recurring revenue, low overhead to start, and a built-in customer base that returns each week.

Cottage Food Business

Origin: USA Food Production
Food Production

Baking, candy making, jams, and preserves operate legally from a home kitchen in most states under cottage food laws. No commercial space required to start. Farmers markets, online orders, and local delivery are the primary sales channels.

Economics

Startup cost is often under $500. Sales caps vary by state under cottage food law, commonly $25,000 to $75,000 per year before a commercial kitchen is required.
