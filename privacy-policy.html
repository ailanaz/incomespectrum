Privacy Policy

How IncomeSpectrum.com and the Income Spectrum App handle data.

Last updated: March 30, 2026

This privacy policy applies to IncomeSpectrum.com (the "Site") and the Income Spectrum App (the "App"), operated by Income Spectrum. It describes what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights in relation to it.

Information We Collect

Income Spectrum does not require account creation, registration, or login. We do not collect your name, email address, or any identifying personal information through the Site or App directly.

The following non-personal data may be collected automatically:

  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Device type, browser type, and operating system
  • General geographic region (country or region level)
  • Referring URLs and navigation paths

This data is collected through Google Analytics and is used only to understand how the Site is used in aggregate. It is not tied to any individual identity.

The Income Spectrum App

The Income Spectrum App stores data locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This includes any ideas, notes, or Founder File content you save within the App. This data does not leave your device and is not transmitted to Income Spectrum or any third party.

Clearing your browser cache or app data will remove any locally stored content. Income Spectrum is not responsible for data loss resulting from clearing local storage.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data. Google may use cookies to collect this information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies

The Site uses cookies set by Google Analytics for anonymous traffic analysis. No other cookies are set by Income Spectrum. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to use the Site or App.

Third-Party Links

Income Spectrum is a directory. It contains links to third-party websites and services. Those sites operate under their own privacy policies. Income Spectrum has no control over and accepts no responsibility for the data practices of any third-party destination linked from this Site.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this Site are affiliate links. Clicking an affiliate link does not transmit any personal information from you to Income Spectrum. Any data collected through a third-party affiliate transaction is governed by that third party's privacy policy.

Data We Do Not Collect or Sell

Income Spectrum does not:

  • Collect personally identifiable information
  • Sell, rent, or trade any user data
  • Use data for advertising targeting
  • Store payment information
  • Share data with third parties beyond Google Analytics

Children

Income Spectrum is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the Site or App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent through our Contact Page or via email at contact@incomespectrum.com.
